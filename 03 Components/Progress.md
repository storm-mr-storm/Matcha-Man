# Progress

**Figma page:** 24 — Status & Progress

Progress communicates **how far along a process is**.

## Circular Progress

- 24×24
- 2px stroke
- Starts at 12 o’clock, clockwise
- Quiet neutral track
- Matcha/primary active arc
- Representative determinate values may include 25/50/75
- Indeterminate uses roughly 25–35% visible arc
- Complete becomes a simple Matcha check rather than a redundant 100% ring
- Error uses the approved negative/error icon treatment

## Progress Bar

- 8px high true capsule
- Matcha fill
- Representative 0/25/50/75/100
- Indeterminate uses a short ~25–35% segment

## Critical rule

Progress amount is not a grade. Low progress is not automatically red and high progress is not automatically green.

Confidence scores, remaining inventory, and chart proportions are not automatically Progress.
