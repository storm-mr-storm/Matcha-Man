# Toggle

**Figma page:** 15 — Toggle

Toggle represents immediate binary on/off state.

## Geometry

- Track: 44×24 true capsule
- Thumb: 18×18 circle
- Inset: 3px

## States

Off/On × Default/Hover/Focus/Disabled.

Off uses quiet neutral; On uses Matcha; Focus uses jazz-blue/info.

## Toggle Field

A common composition places label/description on the left and the switch on the right. Aim for roughly 44px row interaction height.

Do not put `ON`/`OFF` text inside the track.
