# Avatar

**Figma page:** 19 — Avatar

Avatar represents a person, agent, robot, or system identity.

## Sizes

24, 32, 40px. Default is 32px.

## Content

Image, Initials, Icon.

Initials are 1–2 characters, Inter SemiBold, scaled appropriately. Optional status dot sizes may be ~6/8/10px at the bottom-right.

Agent/system identity uses the same Avatar primitive; do not introduce a separate neon “AI avatar” treatment.

Avatar Group MAY use modest overlap when representing multiple identities.
