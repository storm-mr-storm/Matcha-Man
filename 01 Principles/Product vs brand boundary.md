# Product vs brand boundary

Matcha Man has separate product and brand libraries.

## Product library

This vault governs application UI:

- Controls
- Navigation
- Forms
- Data visualization
- Agentic workflows
- Tables and lists
- Status/feedback/progress
- Product surfaces and interactions

## Brand library

The separate brand system governs:

- Marketing pages
- Social assets
- Campaign compositions
- Promotional bento layouts
- Brand-forward illustration and photography usage

## Shared DNA

The two libraries MAY share palette, typography, soft geometry, and overall personality. They SHOULD NOT share component semantics simply because the visual language is related.

A marketing feature card is not automatically the product Card component. A campaign CTA is not automatically evidence that a product Button variant should exist.
