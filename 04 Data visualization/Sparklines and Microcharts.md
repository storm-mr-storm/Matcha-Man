# Sparklines and Microcharts

**Figma page:** 23E — Sparklines & Microcharts

Sparklines communicate trend without chart furniture.

## Defaults

- No axes
- No grid
- No title
- No legend

## Spark Line

~2px stroke.

## Spark Area

~8–12% area fill.

## Spark Bars

~6–10px width, ~4px gap, ~2–3px outer-end radius.

Grouped bars max 2; multi-line max 2.

Key compositions include Metric + Spark and Table Cell + Spark.

Do not automatically make upward movement green or downward movement red. Meaning comes from the metric semantics.
