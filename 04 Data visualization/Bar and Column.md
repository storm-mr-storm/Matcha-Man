# Bar and Column

**Figma page:** 23B — Bar & Column

- Outer-end radius: ~4px
- Baseline edge stays square
- Stacked internal segments remain square where they meet
- Zero baseline is normally required
- Typical column width: ~24–48px
- Grouped series: usually 2–3 max
- Stacked charts only for additive data
- Horizontal bar is preferred for ranking or long category labels

Do not use rounded capsules for ordinary bars; keep the baseline relationship visually precise.
