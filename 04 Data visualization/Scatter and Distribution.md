# Scatter and Distribution

**Figma page:** 23D — Scatter & Distribution

- Default points: ~8px
- Active point: ~12px
- Horizontal and vertical gridlines are useful
- Numeric axes do not require a zero baseline
- Quadrants, reference lines, and trend lines are optional analytical layers

Bubble is an extension. Bubble **area**, not radius, should encode magnitude. Typical bubble sizes may range ~8–32px.
