# Donut and Proportion

**Figma page:** 23C — Donut & Proportion

Prefer Donut over Pie when a useful center metric exists.

- Inner radius: roughly 60%
- 2–5 categories ideal
- Start around 12 o’clock and proceed clockwise
- No exploded slices
- No 3D
- Center value MAY show a key total/percentage

Use categorical colors unless the slices truly represent semantic status.
