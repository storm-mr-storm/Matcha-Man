# Line and Area

**Figma page:** 23A — Line & Area

- Line stroke: ~2px
- Points hidden by default; active point ~6px
- Area fill: roughly 10–16%
- Avoid excessive smoothing that implies false intermediate values
- Prefer 1–3 series
- Typical grid divisions: ~3–5
- Line axes do not need to start at zero when the data story benefits from a tighter domain

Use direct, legible comparison over decorative glow or heavy fills.
