# ADR-007 Dark neutral tail

## Context

The earlier dark theme risked reading as generic charcoal SaaS with a green accent.

## Decision

Use a deeper olive/warm neutral tail culminating in #050705, with semantic dark surfaces mapped across neutral/850–1000.

## Rationale

The target feeling is black coffee + matcha + cream rather than neutral-gray dashboard chrome.

## Consequences

Dark surfaces should remain near-black but retain subtle warm/green character.
